## Core Capabilities and Integrated Technology Portfolio
EDF Power Solutions NA distinguishes itself through a deliberately integrated and technology-agnostic approach. Rather than focusing on a single generation source, the company leverages its deep expertise across a complementary suite of renewable and grid-stabilizing technologies. This integration is key to solving the modern energy trifecta: decarbonization, reliability, and affordability.

**Onshore and Offshore Wind:** With a proven track record spanning decades, EDFps NA is a prominent force in wind energy. Recent orders, such as the 124 MW Haute Chaudière project and the 274 MW Madawaska project in Canada with turbine supplier Vestas, underscore their active expansion and the trust placed in their development and operational prowess. Offshore wind represents the next frontier, and EDF's global leadership in this sector positions its North American arm to play a defining role in harnessing coastal wind resources.

**Solar Photovoltaic (PV):** Utility-scale solar is a cornerstone of the company's strategy. Their projects are designed for maximum efficiency and grid integration. The solar portfolio is increasingly coupled with storage, creating dispatchable clean energy assets that can provide power even when the sun isn't shining.

**Energy Storage:** Recognizing that the future grid must be flexible, EDF Power Solutions NA has made battery energy storage systems (BESS) a central component of its offerings. Storage is the critical enabler that mitigates the intermittency of renewables, provides essential grid services like frequency regulation, and ensures capacity during peak demand periods.

**Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Infrastructure:** As transportation electrifies, the demand for robust, high-capacity charging networks explodes. EDFps NA is building this essential infrastructure, facilitating the transition to electric fleets and personal vehicles, and creating new nodal points of energy management within the grid.

### The Strategic Advantage of Multi-Technology Integration
The true competitive edge of EDF Power Solutions NA lies not in the technologies individually, but in their synergistic combination. A "solar-plus-storage" project, like the landmark Desert Quartzite facility, is more valuable than the sum of its parts. This integration allows for:
* **Enhanced Grid Reliability:** Storage smooths solar output and can inject power during evening peaks.

* **Customized Solutions for Clients:** For corporate or municipal clients seeking to meet 24/7 clean energy goals, a blended portfolio of wind, solar, and storage can be tailored to match specific load profiles far more effectively than a single resource.

## Spotlight on Strategic Projects and Market Impact
Concrete projects demonstrate capability more than any mission statement. EDF Power Solutions NA's growing portfolio across North America provides a clear window into its execution strategy and market impact.

**Desert Quartzite Solar+Storage (California):** This project, developed in partnership with Power Sustainable Energy Infrastructure, is a flagship example of integrated, large-scale clean energy. Located in Riverside County, it boasts a capacity of 375 MWdc of solar combined with a 150 MWac / 4-hour (600 MWh) battery energy storage system. Upon completion, it will provide enough clean, dispatchable power for approximately 117,000 homes and significantly contribute to California's renewable portfolio and grid resilience goals.

**Canadian Wind Expansion (Haute Chaudière & Madawaska):** The consecutive Vestas turbine orders for projects in Canada signal a robust pipeline and a commitment to the Canadian market. These projects bring substantial investment, local clean energy jobs, and tax revenue to their host communities while helping provinces meet their decarbonization targets.

**Ann Arbor's Sustainable Energy Utility:** Cited in the company's materials, this initiative exemplifies EDFps NA's role in municipal partnerships. Working with cities to design and implement localized, sustainable energy utilities allows municipalities to take direct control of their energy future, reduce costs for residents, and accelerate local climate action plans.
